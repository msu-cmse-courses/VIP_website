VIP Advisors

Meet the faculty, staff, and industry professionals who guide our VIP teams.

Dr. Jon Keune
Department of Religious Studies, Michigan State University
keunejon@msu.edu

Link to VIP Team page: ICSAS

Dr. Jon Keune is an Associate Professor and Associate Chair of the Religious Studies Department at MSU, as well as a co-founder of the Regional Bhakti Scholars Network and core faculty in the Digital Humanities @ MSU program. He works especially with Marathi and other Asian texts to study social history and change, which involves collaborating with specialists in other languages, creating new digital corpora, and leveraging digital tools. Working extensively in community-engaged scholarship and nonprofit leadership, Dr. Keune highly values scholarship and teaching that extends beyond the walls of academia and is of use to non-academic communities. He works extensively with mentoring students and teaching independent studies around students’ areas of interest.

Dr. Craig Grosch
Institute for Cyber-Enabled Research (ICER), Michigan State University
grosscra@msu.edu

Link to VIP Team page: ICER

Dr. Grosch is a research computing facilitator at ICER and works to make research computing more accessible. His background is in applied mathematics and computer science, and his Ph.D. research is in developing fast and provably correct algorithms for solving high-dimensional problems. He is very excited to start a new research direction in sustainable computing!

Dr. Tianyi Kou-Herrema
Digital Humanities@MSU, College of Arts and Letters, Michigan State University
koutiany@msu.edu

Link to VIP Team page: TOKEN Lab

Dr. Tianyi Kou-Herrema is the Digital Humanities research coordinator at Michigan State University. Her background is in integrating digital and computational methods to study humanities data. She is passionate about building this interdisciplinary team that studies how we as humans deal with evolving technology that can sometimes feel intimidating. In the next few years, she hopes to become better at understanding and explaining math, a subject she feared in elementary school after being told she was bad at it.

Jonathan Barber
Data Services Librarian, Digital Scholarship Lab, MSU Libraries, Michigan State University
barber85@msu.edu

Link to VIP Team page: TOKEN Lab

Jonathan is the Data Services Librarian at Michigan State University. His background is in data analytics, data governance, data management, and all aspects of research data methodologies. He has worked both in libraries and information technology in higher education for over 20 years and has watched the evolution of technology and its use expand rapidly in the teaching, learning, and research space. Currently he is heavily focused on the use of AI in the research process and how researchers, faculty, and students can learn about, interact with, and utilize these tools in a thoughtful and ethical manner.

Dr. Junlin Yuan
Department of Mechanical Engineering, College of Engineering, Michigan State University
junlin@egr.msu.edu

Link to VIP Team page: TOKEN Lab

Dr. Junlin Yuan is an Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Michigan State University. She studies turbulence, the chaotic swirling of fluids like air and water, with computer simulations and physics-based models. Her research has been supported by NSF, ONR, and industry. She also uses AI in her research and teaching. With Dr. Kou-Herrema and Jonathan Barber, she leads the Spartan Agentic AI Studio, a free course where MSU students build teams of AI agents.

Dr. Phoebe Zarnetske
Department of Integrative Biology, Michigan State University
plz@msu.edu

Link to VIP Team page: SpaCE Lab

The Zarnetske Spatial and Community Ecology Lab (SpaCE Lab) uses a combination of observational data, experiments, and statistical and theoretical modeling to connect observed patterns of biodiversity and community composition with underlying mechanisms across local to global scales. We aim to understand and predict how the composition and geographic distributions of species and ecological communities are affected by biotic interactions, species invasions, biophysical feedbacks, geodiversity, climate change, and land-use change. A central goal is to understand which species and ecological communities are most sensitive and/or resilient to climate change, and in turn act as 'biotic multipliers' of climate change through their outsized impacts on ecological communities.

Jeff Siarto
UXMS Interim Director, College of Communications Arts and Sciences
siartoje@msu.edu

Link to VIP Team page: DUX Lab

Jeff Siarto is a Professor of Practice in the Department of Media and Information at Michigan State University and currently serves as the Interim Director of the MSU User Experience (UXMS) program. Dr. Dirk Colbry
Department of Computational Mathematics, Science and Engineering, Michigan State University
colbrydi@msu.edu

Link to VIP Team page: SEE-Insight

Dr. Dirk Colbry is a Senior Specialist in the Department of Computational Mathematics, Science and Engineering at Michigan State University. He has a background in computer science with expertise in machine vision, pattern recognition, and scientific image analysis. Dr. Mengyan Ma
Department of Advertising and Public Relations
mamengya@msu.edu

Link to VIP Team page: Health Communication & Community Research

Dr. Mengyan Ma is an Assistant Professor (Professor of Practice) in the Department of Advertising and Public Relations at Michigan State University with an active research agenda. Before rejoining MSU, she served as a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Communication and Journalism at University of Wisconsin–Eau Claire from 2021 to 2024. Her research examines the intersection of health, technology, and family communication using mixed methods approaches. As principal investigator, Mengyan received a Trifecta Grant to support interdisciplinary research on influencer marketing of cannabis-infused beverages, with the goal of informing media literacy campaigns, family education, public health initiatives, and regulatory discussions. Her scholarship has been published in journals including Telemedicine and e-Health, International Journal of Communication, Social Media + Society, Health Communication, Journal of Family Communication, Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, and Journal of Advertising Education, among others.
